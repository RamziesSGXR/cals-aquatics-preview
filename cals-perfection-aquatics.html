About us

The stress-free way to keep water clear.

At Cal's Perfection Aquatics, our goal is simple: we take the stress out of aquarium maintenance. Starting with a passion for pristine, thriving aquariums, our expertise quickly expanded to include ponds, water features, and diverse aquatic environments. We genuinely love seeing our clients' smiles when they experience the beauty and tranquility of their healthy aquatic setups. From freshwater to saltwater, we've got you covered — let us show you the true potential of your aquatic space.

Serving Spokane, Coeur d'Alene, Cheney, Airway Heights, Elk, and the surrounding area.

What we do

Every kind of tank gets the royal treatment.

Comprehensive aquarium, pond, and water feature cleanings
Water quality testing and management

Setup and teardown of aquatic environments
