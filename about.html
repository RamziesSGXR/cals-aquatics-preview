Cal's Perfection Aquatics is run by Cameron Latimer. It started with a passion for pristine, thriving aquariums, and grew to cover ponds, water features, and just about every kind of aquatic setup in between.

The goal is simple: take the stress out of aquarium maintenance. Freshwater, brackish, or saltwater, Cameron handles the cleaning, the water testing, and the regular upkeep so you can just enjoy the view.

The best part of the job is the moment a client sees their tank the way it was always meant to look.
