Questions people ask

What areas do you serve?

Spokane, Spokane Valley, Coeur d'Alene, Cheney, Airway Heights, Elk, and the surrounding area. If you're nearby and not sure, just ask.

How much does a tank cleaning cost?

Pricing depends on the size of the aquarium, pond, or water feature, the distance traveled, and the level of maintenance needed. You'll get full pricing with your free consultation.

Do you work on saltwater tanks?

Yes. Freshwater, brackish, and saltwater tanks are all covered.

Do you clean ponds and water features?

Yes. Ponds and water features get the same comprehensive cleaning as aquariums.

Can you take care of my tank while I'm on vacation?

Yes. Vacation aquarium care keeps your fish looked after while you're away.

Can you set up a new tank or take one down?

Yes. Setup and teardown of aquariums and other aquatic setups are part of the service list.

How often should my tank be cleaned?

It depends on the size of the tank, how many fish live in it, and the filtration. During your consultation you'll get a maintenance schedule that fits your setup.

How do I use a code from the tank game?

Add it to your cleaning request. If you won it on this device, the request form fills it in for you.
